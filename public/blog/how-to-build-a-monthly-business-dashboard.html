How to Build a Monthly Business Dashboard for Better Decisions

A practical dashboard architecture for owners who want fewer vanity metrics and faster decisions on cash, margin, sales and execution.

A monthly dashboard should answer three questions: what happened, why it happened and what management will do next. If it only displays numbers, it is a report. If it changes priorities and ownership, it is a management system.

Keep the first version small: cash balance, collections, revenue, gross margin, qualified pipeline, conversion, delivery capacity and the most important operational exception. Each metric needs a definition, owner, source and review frequency.

Databox is a candidate for businesses that want dashboards across multiple systems. Notion can support the commentary layer: assumptions, decisions, action owners and source links. Use one tool for visualisation and one for context, not competing versions of the same KPI.

Set a monthly rhythm: close the period, reconcile the source data, explain material variances, agree actions and record the decision. Restrict edit access, date refreshes, preserve snapshots and document manual adjustments.
