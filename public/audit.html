FAQ04

Questions before
you book.

What do you need from us?

Access to process owners, a shortlist of workflows under pressure, and honest volume/cycle-time context. You do not need a polished data lake — you need clarity on how work actually runs.

Is this a tool selection exercise?

No. Tools come after process and ownership. The Audit decides whether to build, buy or wait — then names the next step.

What happens after the two weeks?

You leave with a scored roadmap. Many clients continue into implementation or team enablement; some execute internally. Either path is fine if the prioritisation is clear.

Can this stay remote?

Yes. Remote interviews and screen-shares work well. On-site days can be scoped when the process is heavily paper- or floor-based.

How do I start?

Book a discovery call, or message on WhatsApp mentioning the Audit. Expect a reply within one business day.
