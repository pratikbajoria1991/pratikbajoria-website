Why a diagnostic firstWhy

Fund the first win,
not the fifth pilot.

Most leaders I speak to are not short of AI ideas. They are short of a defensible answer to a simpler question: which one should we fund first, and how will we know it worked? Without that answer, the default is a pilot chosen by whoever saw the best demo.

The numbers say that default is expensive. Gartner predicted that at least 30% of generative AI projects would be abandoned after proof of concept by the end of 2025, citing poor data quality, inadequate risk controls, escalating costs or unclear business value. McKinsey's 2025 global survey found that 88% of respondents report regular AI use in at least one business function, but only about a third say their organisations have begun to scale it. Finance is no exception: Gartner found 58% of finance functions using AI in 2024.

The Audit exists to close that gap before money is committed. As a Chartered Accountant, I treat it the way I would treat any investment decision: baseline first, controls designed in, and a written reason to build, buy or wait.

What is an AI Opportunity Audit?

An AI Opportunity Audit is a fixed-fee, two-week diagnostic that maps workflows, scores AI use cases on impact, effort and risk, and delivers a prioritised 90-day roadmap before a build is funded.

What do you need from us?

Access to process owners, a shortlist of workflows under pressure, and honest volume/cycle-time context. You do not need a polished data lake — you need clarity on how work actually runs.

Is this a tool selection exercise?

No. Tools come after process and ownership. The Audit decides whether to build, buy or wait — then names the next step.

What happens after the two weeks?

You leave with a scored roadmap. Many clients continue into implementation or team enablement; some execute internally. Either path is fine if the prioritisation is clear.

Can this stay remote?

Yes. Remote interviews and screen-shares work well. On-site days can be scoped when the process is heavily paper- or floor-based.

How do I start?

Book a discovery call, or message on WhatsApp mentioning the Audit. Expect a reply within one business day.
