Why pilots stall01

Automation without owners
does not ship.

Mid-market ops teams often buy a platform or spin up a chatbot before naming the bottleneck. Without volumes, hand-offs and a stop/continue rule, work stays in “pilot”. AI helps when it compresses a real queue — documents, exceptions, routing, CRM/ERP updates — under an accountable owner.

FAQ03

Questions COOs
ask before funding.

What is workflow automation with AI for mid-market companies?

Using AI and integrations to reduce hand-offs, cycle time and rework on owned operational workflows — document intake, exception queues, WhatsApp/email routing, CRM or ERP updates — with clear owners and stop rules, not unsupervised company-wide chatbots.

Where should a COO start?

Pick one bottleneck with volume and an owner: lead qualification, document processing, order exceptions, support triage or internal reporting. Map the real hand-offs before selecting tools.

Build, buy or wait — how do we decide?

Score each use case on business impact, implementation effort and risk. Buy when a product fits the process; build when differentiation or integration depth matters; wait when data, ownership or governance is not ready.

How do we avoid pilots that never reach production?

Require a process owner, a metric, a definition of done and a 90-day stop/continue rule before funding. A structured AI Opportunity Audit produces that ranked roadmap.
