## From ambiguity *to shipped.*

A deliberately unglamorous four-stage engagement. No pilots that never leave the lab, no decks that gather dust.

STEP 01

### Diagnose

Two weeks inside your operations. I map workflows, interview your team and quantify where time and margin are leaking.

STEP 02

### Prioritise

Every candidate use case scored on business impact, implementation effort and risk. You get a ranked roadmap, not a wish list.

STEP 03

### Build

We ship the highest-value use case first, in weeks rather than quarters. Real system, real users, real measurement.

STEP 04

### Embed

Training, documentation and handover so the capability outlives the engagement. Optional retainer for the next horizon.
