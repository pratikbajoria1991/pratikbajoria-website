# Privacy in plain English.

> Privacy notice for pratikbajoria.com: what the discovery-call, cross-border and newsletter forms store, who can see it, and how to ask for deletion.

Canonical: https://pratikbajoria.com/privacy
Last updated: 2026-09-18

When you submit a discovery-call request or a cross-border partnership enquiry, or subscribe to the AI Implementation Brief, this site stores the information you provide — such as your name, email, company, phone number and message — in a private Cloudflare D1 database so Pratik can respond and manage the relationship.

Access to submissions is restricted to the site owner. The site does not sell your information. Information may be shared with a service provider only when needed to operate the website or respond to you. You can request access, correction or deletion by emailing [hello@pratikbajoria.com](mailto:hello@pratikbajoria.com).

WhatsApp and email buttons open your chosen external service with a pre-filled message. Their handling of information is governed by their own privacy policies. Affiliate partners may use cookies or tracking parameters when you follow a disclosed link.

To understand how the site is used, pages load Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity after the page has displayed. They record usage such as pages viewed, clicks and scrolling; Clarity may also provide heatmaps and session replays. Form fields are masked and form contents are not intentionally sent to these analytics services. See the [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement).

Disha uses your browser's speech recognition and speech synthesis after you activate the microphone. This website does not store your audio or conversation transcript; speech processing may be handled by your browser provider. Disha analytics record interaction events such as opening, listening, speaking, categorized question intent and conversation completion, not the spoken or typed question itself.

If you arrive with campaign tags (UTM, Google click ID or Meta click ID), first-touch attribution is kept in session storage and attached to a form submission in Cloudflare D1. When the optional Google Sheets integration is configured, submitted lead details and attribution are also mirrored to the site owner's private reporting sheet. D1 remains the primary lead store.

Google Analytics 4 may receive pseudonymous usage events such as page engagement, scroll milestones, article interactions, categorized blog-search intent and Core Web Vitals. Collection depends on browser settings, privacy controls and network conditions.

This concise notice should be reviewed with the final email provider, analytics configuration and applicable Indian privacy obligations before a paid acquisition campaign or large-scale email programme launches.
