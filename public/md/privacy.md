# Privacy in plain English.

> Privacy notice for pratikbajoria.com: what the discovery-call, cross-border and newsletter forms store, who can see it, and how to ask for deletion.

Canonical: https://pratikbajoria.com/privacy
Last updated: 2026-09-18

When you submit a discovery-call request or a cross-border partnership enquiry, or subscribe to the AI Implementation Brief, this site stores the information you provide — such as your name, email, company, phone number and message — in a private Cloudflare D1 database so Pratik can respond and manage the relationship.

Access to submissions is restricted to the site owner. The site does not sell your information. Information may be shared with a service provider only when needed to operate the website or respond to you. You can request access, correction or deletion by emailing [hello@pratikbajoria.com](mailto:hello@pratikbajoria.com).

WhatsApp and email buttons open your chosen external service with a pre-filled message. Their handling of information is governed by their own privacy policies. Affiliate partners may use cookies or tracking parameters when you follow a disclosed link.

To understand how the site is used, pages load Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity after the page has displayed. They record anonymised usage such as pages viewed, clicks, scrolling and, in Clarity's case, heatmaps and session replays; form fields are masked and no names, emails or form text are sent to them. Microsoft's handling is described in the [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement).

The voice agent uses your browser's speech recognition and speech synthesis capabilities after you press the microphone button. This website does not store your audio or transcript; browser speech processing may be handled by your browser provider.

This concise notice should be reviewed with the final email provider, analytics configuration and applicable Indian privacy obligations before a paid acquisition campaign or large-scale email programme launches.
