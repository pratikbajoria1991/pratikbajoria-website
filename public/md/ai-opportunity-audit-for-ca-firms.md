Why CA firms 01

## Scale delivery without *scaling headcount blindly.*

Practices sit on document-heavy, deadline-driven work. AI can compress intake, extraction and first-draft research — but only when ownership, review gates and confidentiality are designed in. A generic chatbot pilot rarely survives partner scrutiny; a scored roadmap does.

01 / MAP

### Delivery & back-office mapping

Walk compliance calendars, engagement packs, reconciliation routines and client paperwork with the people who own the work.

02 / SCORE

### Use cases with controls

Score candidates on impact, effort and risk — including review gates, logging and what must remain human-signed.

03 / DECIDE

### Build / buy / wait

Clear calls per use case. Tooling follows process and ownership — not the other way around.

04 / ROADMAP

### 90-day priorities

A sequenced plan for the first quarter: what ships, who owns it, and what “done” looks like in cycle time and error rates.

Fit check 02

## Who this helps *— and who it does not.*

### For

- Managing partners and practice leads who need a ranked list of AI bets, not a tool tour
- Firms under pressure to cut pack turnaround or exception queues without adding unchecked headcount
- Teams ready to keep professional judgement with partners while automating throughput
- Practices willing to share real volumes, systems and confidentiality constraints

### Not for

- Shopping for an unsupervised “AI that signs opinions”
- Firms unwilling to discuss client-data handling or review gates
- Engagements that need a multi-month strategy study before any decision
- Buyers seeking the cheapest tool list without process ownership

## Questions CA partners *ask before booking.*

### What is an AI Opportunity Audit for a CA firm?

A fixed-fee two-week diagnostic that maps your delivery and back-office workflows, scores AI use cases on impact, effort and risk (including auditability), and produces a prioritised 90-day roadmap. See the [Audit package](https://pratikbajoria.com/audit).

### Which CA firm workflows are usually in scope?

Document intake and extraction, compliance calendar and filing support, reconciliation packs, research and memo drafting with review gates, client onboarding paperwork, and internal MIS — always with human ownership of conclusions.

### Will this replace professional judgement?

No. The Audit is designed so AI assists throughput and consistency while partners and managers keep responsibility for opinions, filings and client advice.

### How is client confidentiality handled?

Architecture, retention and access controls are scoped to your firm’s policies. Sensitive work can use redaction, private deployments and audit trails — agreed before any build.
