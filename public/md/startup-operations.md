### What does startup operations support include?

The scope is customized. It can include workspace search, coordination with specialist business-service providers, finance and operating workflows, and AI-enabled process automation. The plan identifies who delivers each item and what it costs.

### Does Pratik personally deliver every service?

Pratik directly leads process discovery, operating workflow design and automation work. Workspace, legal, entity, hiring, branding or specialist IT services are coordinated with the relevant provider. The provider, responsibilities and fees are agreed before work begins.

### Can the plan be tailored to our startup?

Yes. The recommended scope depends on your startup stage, city, team size, budget, priorities and timeline. Share those details during an introductory conversation.

### Can you arrange office space in any city?

Workspace options depend on the requested city, budget, size and current provider availability. Availability and commercial terms are confirmed before you make a decision.

### How are fees handled when a specialist provider is involved?

The written scope should identify each provider, their deliverable and fee, and any referral or coordination fee before the engagement starts.
